Who Am I

I am Kulshreshth Arora, based in Haryana, INDIA. My name literally translates to "Best in the entire extended family." It is a heavy title to carry, but it serves as a constant reminder of the standard I need to uphold—whether I am untangling a complex philosophy structure or navigating my second semester at MM(DU).

Optimistic Nihilism

I view life through the lens of Optimistic Nihilism. The realization that there is no inherent, predefined meaning to the universe isn't depressing to me—it is liberating. It removes the paralyzing pressure of living up to artificial cosmic expectations. It means I am free to dictate my own purpose, build my own systems, and define what matters.

Continuous Growth

Beyond writing code, I am deeply invested in understanding human nature and self-mastery. Whether I am digesting 101 Essays That Will Change The Way You Think, analyzing classic Russian literature, or pulling late-night collaborative study sessions with friends, I believe that building a great life requires the same intentional architecture as building great software.

Things That I Believe In

  • ♥︎  Your choices define you, not your circumstances.
